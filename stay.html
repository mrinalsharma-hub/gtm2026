Nainital sits at 2,084 meters, nested around a pear-shaped, natural freshwater lake. Here is the easiest way to reach us.

BY AIR

Pantnagar (PGH): The closest runway (~2 hours away by road / 65 km). Direct daily domestic flights from New Delhi.

New Delhi (DEL): Fly into Delhi, then take a train or private taxi. This is the most frequent and reliable option.

BY RAIL

Kathgodam (KGM) is the nearest railway station — about 23 km and a scenic one-hour taxi drive up the hill.

Direct Trains: Overnight and day trains run from Delhi (Ranikhet Express, Kathgodam Shatabdi Express).

Local Transit: Pre-paid taxis and cabs are readily available at the station exit.

BY ROAD

From Delhi, it is roughly 300 km (6.5 to 8 hours) via Moradabad, Rampur, Bazpur, and Haldwani.

Hill Drive: The final 35 km ascends winding mountain roads. Keep motion-sickness tablets handy if you need them.

WEATHER & PACKING

Expect crisp, sunny mountain days around 10–15 °C and chilly evenings dipping to 2–7 °C.

What to pack: Heavy woollens, a warm evening coat or shawl, and comfortable walking shoes — Nainital is best explored on foot.
