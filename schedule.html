WEDDING WEEKEND

NOV 20

FRIDAY  |  BY 6 PM

Arrive in Nainital

Take the evening to settle in, refresh, and soak up the mountain air. We’ve kept tonight entirely free so you can unwind at your own pace after your journey.

FRIDAY  |  BRIDE'S 5PM, GROOM'S 7:30PM

Mehndi

While traditional Kumaoni weddings don’t feature grand Mehndi or Sangeet ceremonies, we couldn't resist!

This is a completely optional event but if you are game, join us for a cozy, informal henna session—especially for the ladies!

Venue:  Bride's side: Home address, Groom's side: TBC

Dress code:  Dress casually and comfortably in whatever you like—and feel free to dress up if you wish—but do bring a warm layer as Nainital nights in November get quite chilly.

NOV 21

SATURDAY  |  10 AM ONWARDS

Day Wedding

Venue:  Balrampur House Nainital, About the venue

Dress code:  We’d love for you to join in the colorful spirit of our Kumaoni wedding! Guests are highly encouraged to wear traditional Indian wear (like a Kurta, Lehenga, or Salwar Suit) or the traditional attire of your own culture. If you prefer, colorful Western formal wear is also completely welcome. We just ask that you avoid wearing red (reserved for the bride), black, or white to the main ceremony. In case you have questions or need help, please reach out to us!

Tentative plan:

10:00 AM Assembly of Baraat:  Groom's wedding procession.

11:30 AM Varmaala:  Bride and groom exchange garlands.

01:00 PM Pheras & Saptapadi:  Sacred vows and ceremonies.

4:30 PM Vidaai:  Heartfelt farewell of bride.

Evening Party
